Authority: Volume 8 Appendix A (p. 164) governs ids ("The 108 stable product capabilities …"); Volume 8 ADR-004 (p. 205); Volume 9 Appendix N (p. 186) binds chapters to capabilities; `audit/CROSS_REFERENCES.md` §2 and §5 item 4. Rules. (1) The historical id stays the row id in `audit/requirements/v09.csv` and in every unit's `source_rows`; nothing is renamed. (2) The `cap_alias` column of the requirement CSVs holds `defined` for an id in Appendix A and `alias-v1:<target>[+<target>]` for an aliased id; `audit/second-pass.mjs` fills it from this table and `audit/summarise-units.mjs` refuses a V9 CAP binding that resolves nowhere. (3) A change of alias is a new table version (v2 …) with the old row kept and marked superseded — a row is never edited or deleted. (4) An alias moves no obligation: the obligations traced by a V9 footer are the `PR-CC-001..006` rows of the V8 chapters it names (column "V8 chapters traced"), which keep their own rows and units; the alias only names the capability those obligations belong to. (5) Volume 9 needs an amendment replacing the 18 ids (rows UX-01-001..006 note); until then the alias is the resolution.
