##   Preamble
Strategic failure rarely begins in the meeting where a decision is made. It begins earlier: evidence is fragmented, relationships are invisible, assumptions are undocumented, uncertainty is compressed into false certainty, and institutional memory disappears between teams and leadership cycles.
The Eye exists to change that operating condition. It creates a persistent, connected, explainable system through which an organization can observe its environment and itself; understand the forces shaping outcomes; predict plausible futures across multiple horizons; simulate interventions before committing resources; decide with accountable human judgment; and learn from the consequences.
This is not a promise of omniscience. The Eye must know what it knows, show why it believes it, disclose what remains uncertain, and preserve the evidence required for challenge. Its value is not generated by producing more answers. Its value is generated by improving the quality, speed, continuity, and accountability of strategic decisions.
The system is designed for institutions whose choices affect markets, infrastructure, public services, national interests, communities, capital, supply chains, health, security, and long-term competitiveness. The standard is therefore higher than convenience software. Trust, provenance, human control, security, resilience, sovereignty, and institutional memory are part of the product itself.

## 1  Purpose, Authority, and Use
The Product Constitution is the control point for coherence. It separates durable commitments from choices that engineering teams are expected to improve over time.
The Eye will span a large product surface: external observation, enterprise integration, data processing, graph intelligence, models, agents, digital twins, forecasting, simulation, executive workflows, and governed marketplaces. Without a constitutional baseline, each surface would tend to invent its own language and optimize locally. The resulting product could be technically sophisticated and strategically incoherent. The product category, mission, canonical descriptor, and operating doctrine.

## 2  Mission, Category, and Doctrine
The Eye is the operating system through which an institution continuously converts a changing world into accountable strategic action and institutional learning.
## Mission
The Eye continuously observes the external and internal world, understands relationships between events and entities, builds organizational memory, predicts possible futures, simulates strategic decisions, and provides explainable recommendations for human decision-makers.
## The six operating verbs
Observe. Acquire and preserve evidence about external and internal change with source identity, time, rights, quality, and chain of custody.
Understand. Convert evidence into entities, events, claims, relationships, patterns, hypotheses, and causal structures while preserving uncertainty and competing interpretations.
Predict. Estimate future states, events, indicators, risks, and opportunities across the six canonical horizons using calibrated, explainable methods.
Simulate. Test interventions and external shocks against grounded Digital Twins and scenario assumptions before resources, authority, or lives are committed.
Decide. Present humans with traceable options, consequences, trade-offs, uncertainty, and recommended actions aligned to explicit objectives and constraints.
Learn. Compare expectation with outcome, preserve the decision record, update memory, evaluate models and agents, and improve the system through governed release processes.
## Category definition
A Strategic Intelligence Operating System is a persistent enterprise system that coordinates data, knowledge, models, agents, twins, scenarios, simulations, decisions, and learning around a common representation of the world and the organization. The operating system metaphor is functional: The Eye provides shared services, permissions, memory, workflows, policy, execution controls, and interaction surfaces upon which domain solutions operate.
A chatbot can answer a question. A dashboard can display a metric. A BI platform can aggregate historic data. A copilot can assist a user. The Eye may include all of those interaction patterns, but its defining capability is the continuous, governed strategic loop that persists across questions, teams, systems, and leadership cycles.

## 3  Product Principles and Prohibitions
The product principles are design tests. When priorities conflict, they determine which compromise is acceptable and which would change the nature of the product.
## Founding principles
Observe Everything. The platform shall be able to incorporate any lawful, authorized, relevant source class while making coverage limits visible.
Understand Before Predicting. Prediction shall be grounded in entities, relationships, events, context, mechanisms, and temporal state—not pattern matching detached from meaning.
Explain Every Recommendation. A decision-maker must be able to inspect evidence, assumptions, drivers, uncertainty, alternatives, and provenance.

Humans Decide. The system informs and prepares decisions; accountable people retain final authority.
Learn Continuously. Outcomes and feedback shall improve memory, models, workflows, and policy through controlled learning loops.
Remember Permanently. Institutional knowledge shall persist by default within lawful retention, deletion, classification, and customer-governance boundaries.
Protect by Design. Security, privacy, sovereignty, safety, and auditability are architectural properties.
Enterprise First. Governance, lifecycle management, reliability, interoperability, and deployment control are baseline requirements, not later upgrades.
## Constitutional prohibitions
The product shall not present inference, synthetic data, or scenario output as observed fact.
The product shall not conceal material uncertainty to produce a cleaner executive narrative.
The product shall not make a recommendation without showing which objectives, constraints, evidence, and assumptions shaped it.
The product shall not allow agents, models, or workflows to accumulate undeclared authority through convenience or repeated use.
The product shall not create separate, irreconcilable truths for executives, analysts, operators, and governance teams.
The product shall not make lock-in a precondition for preserving or exporting customer knowledge.

## 4  Customers and Domain Applicability
The Eye is universal in applicability and enterprise in operating standard. The shared core remains stable while domains express their own ontologies, models, policies, twins, and decision workflows.
## Customer universe
The platform is designed for governments, Fortune 500 companies, defence organizations, banks, healthcare institutions, manufacturers, energy companies, logistics networks, telecom operators, retailers, consulting firms, and small and medium-sized enterprises. This breadth does not justify a lowest-common-denominator product. It requires a strong common substrate and governed specialization.
## Common core
All customers share the same fundamental objects: sources, evidence, entities, events, claims, relationships, objectives, risks, opportunities, capabilities, indicators, hypotheses, scenarios, forecasts, simulations, decisions, actions, outcomes, and lessons. Shared semantics allow the platform to transfer product capability across sectors without transferring private customer knowledge.
## Domain specialization
Each sector may add domain packs containing ontology extensions, source connectors, policies, metrics, twin templates, model suites, agent capabilities, workflow patterns, simulation methods, and executive views. Extensions must identify their namespace, owner, evidence requirements, evaluation criteria, compatibility range, and lifecycle. They may enrich the core; they may not redefine foundational terms in a way that breaks cross-domain reasoning.
## Organizational scale
Smaller organizations may consume a managed configuration with narrower scope. Large enterprises and governments may operate federated, multi-tenant, multi-classification, multi-region, or disconnected deployments. The intelligence lifecycle, trust model, and core semantics remain the same across scale. Simplicity may be achieved through configuration and experience design, not through removal of accountability.

## 5  Human Authority and Responsible Use
The Eye increases the range and speed of machine-supported reasoning without transferring moral, legal, or strategic accountability away from people.
## Decision sovereignty
Every strategic decision record shall name the accountable human decision authority, the participants who reviewed it, the evidence and recommendations available, the approval state, and any override. The system may collect, transform, monitor, notify, forecast, generate options, run simulations, draft decisions, and execute pre-authorized reversible administrative steps. It may not represent those activities as final human judgment.
## High-impact boundaries
Where a decision materially affects life, liberty, legal status, essential services, employment, credit, health, safety, or comparable rights and interests, the system shall operate in decision-support mode with explicit human review, applicable legal and policy controls, and an auditable basis. The presence of a human click is not sufficient if the workflow withholds material information, coerces approval, or makes challenge impractical.
## Responsible institutional use
Customers remain accountable for lawful purpose, authority, proportionality, data rights, policy, and operational use. The platform shall provide controls that make responsible operation possible: purpose binding, access control, policy enforcement, explainability, audit, contestability, override, retention, review, and shutdown. The Eye shall not claim neutrality when its configuration or outputs materially shape a decision process.
## Explanation without performative transparency
Explainability shall communicate the basis of an output in a form appropriate to the decision. It includes evidence, assumptions, model and agent identity, important drivers, uncertainty, alternatives, limitations, sensitivity, and required verification. It does not require disclosure of protected internal chain-of-thought. A concise reasoning summary and reproducible evidence path are the governing standard.

## 6  The Strategic Intelligence Lifecycle
The Eye is one closed-loop system. Intelligence becomes valuable only when evidence, foresight, decisions, actions, outcomes, and learning remain connected.
Observe. Sources are discovered, authorized, collected, timestamped, preserved, and monitored under explicit coverage and freshness contracts.
Understand. Content and telemetry are cleaned, classified, resolved into entities and events, connected through relationships, summarized, assessed, and stored with uncertainty and provenance.
Predict. Models estimate future states and indicators across the canonical horizons, expose confidence and drivers, and register conditions that would invalidate the forecast.
Simulate. Scenarios and interventions are applied to versioned Digital Twins to estimate direct, indirect, delayed, systemic, and adversarial effects.
Decide. Options are compared against objectives, constraints, risk appetite, policy, stakeholder obligations, reversibility, and information value; humans approve or reject.
Learn. Actions and outcomes are observed, forecast and simulation performance are scored, assumptions are revisited, lessons enter memory, and approved improvements are released.
## Lifecycle continuity
Each stage shall produce versioned objects that the next stage can consume and the previous stage can audit. A recommendation must link backward to scenarios, forecasts, twin state, claims, and evidence. It must link forward to the human decision, authorized action, observed outcome, and resulting lesson. Breaking these links converts the platform into a collection of tools and violates the operating-system category.
## Re-entry and iteration
The lifecycle is not a simple pipeline. New evidence can invalidate an understanding; a simulation can expose missing observations; a decision review can require another scenario; an outcome can change the ontology or source strategy. The workflow model must support controlled iteration while preserving every prior state and reason for change.

## 8  World Observation Layer
Observation is an evidence discipline, not a scraping feature. The layer must know what was observed, from where, when, under what authority, with what quality, and what remains unseen.
## Source universe
The canonical source universe includes governments, regulations, news, financial markets, research, scientific papers, patents, company filings, supply chains, IoT, ERP, CRM, internal documents, satellites, climate data, geopolitical events, social signals, APIs, RSS, and customer-defined sources. Support means more than connection: it includes discovery, authorization, acquisition, change detection, schema understanding, rights enforcement, source health, backfill, replay, and retirement.
## Observation object
Every collected object shall preserve source identity, source type, acquisition method, event time where available, collection time, publication time, effective time, content hash, original payload reference, parser and connector version, licensing or usage restrictions, geographic scope, language, classification, sensitivity, tenant, and quality indicators. The original evidence must remain accessible according to policy even after derived intelligence has been created.
## Coverage intelligence
The layer shall model its own field of view. Source gaps, delayed feeds, failed connectors, regional blind spots, licensing exclusions, sampling bias, sensor degradation, and collection uncertainty are intelligence. Downstream systems must be able to distinguish ‘no event occurred’ from ‘the platform could not observe whether an event occurred.’
## Adversarial environment
External information may be manipulated, duplicated, poisoned, spoofed, selectively released, or generated to influence analysis. Observation workflows shall support source reputation, corroboration, anomaly detection, content authenticity signals, malicious payload isolation, prompt-injection resistance, and escalation to human review.

## 9  Intelligence Layer
The Intelligence Layer turns evidence into structured understanding while preserving the difference between what a source said, what the system inferred, and what a human accepted.
## Core transformations
The layer coordinates collection, cleaning, normalization, language processing, classification, summarization, named-entity recognition, entity resolution, event extraction, relationship extraction, claim extraction, topic and theme analysis, geospatial and temporal alignment, contradiction detection, source comparison, weak-signal detection, and analytical assessment. These transformations may use deterministic rules, statistical models, graph methods, foundation models, specialist models, human review, or combinations of them.
## Intelligence object model
Evidence. A preserved observation obtained from an identified source.
Claim. A proposition asserted by a source, model, analyst, or institution, linked to supporting and contradicting evidence.
Assessment. A time-bounded analytical judgment that synthesizes claims and evidence, with confidence, assumptions, alternatives, and accountable authorship.
Hypothesis. A testable explanation or expectation with indicators that would strengthen, weaken, or falsify it.
Signal. A change or pattern potentially relevant to an objective, risk, opportunity, scenario, or decision.
## Conflict and uncertainty
Conflicting claims shall not be flattened into one convenient answer. The layer shall retain disagreement, identify source dependence, estimate confidence, surface missing evidence, and allow multiple assessments to coexist. Human analysts must be able to endorse, challenge, amend, or supersede an assessment without deleting the historical record.
## Time and revision
Intelligence is provisional. Each object shall declare when it was valid, when it was known, when it was revised, and what evidence caused the revision. This bitemporal discipline enables Decision Replay, prevents hindsight leakage in model evaluation, and makes intelligence evolution auditable.

## 10  Enterprise Memory and Strategic Memory
The Eye must remember more than documents. It must remember context: what the organization knew, believed, assumed, decided, attempted, observed, and learned.
## Enterprise Memory
Enterprise Memory is the governed, durable memory of sources, documents, messages, telemetry, entities, events, claims, analyses, workflows, decisions, actions, and outcomes. It spans structured and unstructured data and preserves access policy, provenance, time, version, classification, and tenant boundaries. Retrieval must be contextual and permission-aware rather than merely lexical.
## Strategic Memory
Strategic Memory is the decision-centric subset and interpretation layer of Enterprise Memory. It preserves objectives, hypotheses, assumptions, scenarios, forecasts, simulation configurations, recommendations, dissent, approvals, commitments, outcomes, post-decision reviews, and lessons. It allows a future team to understand not only what was decided, but why the decision was rational given the evidence available at the time.
## Memory writes
Model output shall not become accepted institutional memory merely because it was generated. Writes shall carry truth state, provenance, confidence, author, review status, expiry, and correction history. High-impact assessments, decisions, and lessons require governed acceptance. Retrieval systems must communicate whether content is observed, inferred, disputed, superseded, simulated, or approved.
## Durability and forgetting
‘Remember Permanently’ means that strategic context does not disappear through application boundaries, staff turnover, or model context limits. It does not override law, contract, consent, security policy, or customer-directed deletion. The platform shall support legal holds, retention schedules, archival tiers, correction, redaction, and verifiable deletion while preserving permitted audit facts about the operation performed.

## 11  Knowledge Graph and Strategy Graph
The Knowledge Graph is the semantic heart of The Eye. It gives every layer a shared, temporal, provenance-rich understanding of what exists, how it is related, and how that understanding changes.
## Knowledge Graph scope
The graph connects companies, countries, governments, products, technologies, people, supply chains, markets, regulations, events, risks, objectives, capabilities, and any governed domain extension. Nodes and relationships are typed, versioned, time-aware, source-linked, confidence-bearing, policy-scoped, and globally identifiable within the appropriate tenant or federation boundary.
## Graph truth model
A graph edge is not automatically a fact. Relationships may be observed, asserted, inferred, predicted, simulated, disputed, or accepted. The graph shall preserve this state, the supporting and contradicting evidence, derivation method, confidence, validity interval, and reviewer status. Identity resolution decisions shall be reversible and merge history shall remain auditable.
## Strategy Graph
The Strategy Graph is a governed strategic overlay anchored to the Knowledge Graph. It connects objectives to indicators, assumptions, risks, opportunities, scenarios, decisions, initiatives, capabilities, resources, owners, dependencies, commitments, and outcomes. It makes strategy executable and inspectable: a change in a supplier, regulation, market, technology, or geopolitical condition can be traced to the objectives and decisions it may affect.

Changes require impact analysis across data mappings, agents, models, twins, policies, simulations, APIs, and user experiences. Domain experts must be able to participate in ontology review without editing storage schemas directly.

## 12  Digital Twins
A Digital Twin is a living, evidence-grounded, versioned model of a strategic system—not a static profile and not a decorative visualization.
## Canonical twin portfolio
Every customer may maintain Digital Twins for the company or institution, competitors, supply chain, products, markets, regulations, infrastructure, business processes, and organization. Twins may be composed: a company twin can reference product, market, workforce, infrastructure, regulatory, and supply-chain twins while preserving ownership and update boundaries.
## Twin anatomy
Identity and scope: the real-world system represented, boundaries, owners, jurisdictions, and intended decisions.
State: current and historical attributes, relationships, events, metrics, capabilities, constraints, and health indicators.
Behavior: rules, causal structures, statistical models, agent behaviors, feedback loops, delays, thresholds, and resource constraints.
Evidence: observations and claims supporting each material state element, with provenance and confidence.
Interfaces: inputs, outputs, dependencies, control variables, policy limits, and composition contracts with other twins.
Validation: calibration history, known limitations, operating envelope, drift status, and approved uses.
## Actual and simulated state
A twin shall distinguish actual observed state, estimated current state, predicted state, and simulated state. Scenario branches must not overwrite the baseline. Users shall be able to compare branches, inspect their assumptions, reconcile them against later observation, and reproduce the model state used in a past decision.
## Twin confidence
Completeness and confidence shall be computed at component level. A polished twin with stale financial data, missing suppliers, or an unvalidated behavioral model is not healthy. The interface must expose blind spots, staleness, model validity, and sensitivity alongside outputs.

## Forecast package
A material forecast shall include the target variable or event, horizon, as-of time, data window, method, model and feature versions, base rate, probability distribution or interval, confidence, calibration history, important drivers, causal assumptions where claimed, scenario dependencies, sensitivity, out-of-distribution warnings, expiry, and conditions that would cause revision.
## Model ensembles and disagreement
The engine may combine time-series, econometric, causal, graph, simulation, machine-learning, expert, and foundation-model approaches. Model disagreement is not an implementation defect to hide; it is strategic information. The system shall show when different credible methods imply different futures and identify which assumptions drive the divergence.
## Evaluation
Forecasts shall be scored after outcomes become observable using horizon-appropriate metrics, calibration curves, baseline comparisons, and segmented error analysis. Evaluation shall prevent hindsight leakage by reconstructing only the information available at forecast time. Performance shall be visible by domain, geography, regime, model, and confidence band.

## 14  Scenario Intelligence
Scenarios are coherent, inspectable possible futures used to challenge strategy. They are not long-form narratives detached from live evidence, and they are not predictions disguised as certainty.
## Scenario anatomy
A scenario shall identify its purpose, scope, horizon, initial conditions, driving forces, critical uncertainties, actors, assumptions, causal logic, events, indicators, branch points, constraints, expected impacts, probability treatment where appropriate, and signals that strengthen or weaken it. It shall link to the entities, relationships, twins, objectives, risks, and opportunities it affects.
## Scenario portfolio
The system shall support baseline, upside, downside, disruption, stress, adversarial, counterfactual, and user-defined scenarios. Scenario portfolios should span the uncertainty space relevant to a decision rather than merely surround a preferred plan with cosmetic alternatives. Minority and expert dissent may be represented as separate scenarios when assumptions differ materially.
## Living scenarios
Scenarios shall remain linked to leading indicators and observed events. As the world changes, the platform updates scenario relevance, notifies owners when assumptions are invalidated, records transitions between branches, and preserves the original edition used for a decision. A scenario may become less plausible without being deleted; its lessons may remain valuable.
## Scenario Marketplace
Governed scenario packages may be published, licensed, adapted, and evaluated across customers without exposing private data. A package contains ontology dependencies, assumptions, models, indicators, evaluation evidence, permissions, and compatibility metadata. Customer instantiation creates a separate governed copy linked to the publisher and version.

## 15  Simulation Engine
Simulation enables decision-makers to intervene in a model before intervening in the world. Its authority depends on reproducibility, validation, and honest limits.
## Simulation classes
The engine shall support deterministic and stochastic simulations, discrete-event and system-dynamics models, agent-based models, Monte Carlo analysis, network propagation, optimization, stress testing, war-gaming, counterfactual analysis, and domain-specific methods. No method is universally preferred; selection shall match the decision, system behavior, data, horizon, and validation evidence.
## Experiment contract
Every run shall identify the twin and scenario versions, initial state, intervention, control case, constraints, policy boundaries, assumptions, model versions, parameters, random seeds, execution environment, outputs, uncertainty, sensitivity, and responsible operator. Runs shall be immutable once completed; corrections produce a new version linked to the original.
## Causal discipline
A simulation may explore consequences without proving causality. The interface shall distinguish mechanistic assumptions, empirically estimated causal effects, correlations, expert judgments, and speculative relationships. When the model crosses its validated operating envelope, results must be marked accordingly and approval thresholds increased.
## Human challenge
Decision-makers and domain experts shall be able to inspect assumptions, modify interventions, compare runs, challenge model structure, and request sensitivity analysis. Simulation results shall never be summarized into a recommendation without retaining access to the experiment and its limitations.

## 16  Decision Intelligence
Decision Intelligence is the constitutional bridge between machine-supported foresight and accountable human choice.
## Decision object
A strategic decision shall be a first-class, versioned object containing question, scope, owner, deadline, objectives, constraints, stakeholders, options, evidence, assumptions, forecasts, scenarios, simulations, risks, opportunities, trade-offs, confidence, dissent, recommendation, approval, conditions, actions, review triggers, and outcome measures. The record captures both the decision and the decision environment.
## Option architecture
The system shall identify viable alternatives, including defer, stage, pilot, acquire information, hedge, partner, exit, and maintain status quo where relevant. Options shall be assessed against explicit objectives and constraints; scoring models must expose weights and sensitivity. The most likely option is not automatically the most valuable, and the highest expected value is not automatically acceptable under risk, rights, or policy constraints.
=== PARA 251 ===
## Recommendation contract
A recommendation shall state what is proposed, for whom, by when, under which assumptions, why it is preferred, which alternatives were considered, what could make it wrong, what evidence is missing, which consequences are material, how reversible it is, and what monitoring or decision triggers follow approval. Confidence in the recommendation shall not exceed confidence in its evidence, twin state, forecasts, and assumptions.
## Decision Replay
Decision Replay reconstructs the exact knowledge state available at decision time: source versions, claims, graph, twin state, forecasts, scenarios, simulations, recommendation, dissent, policy, and approvals. Later outcomes are then overlaid without altering the original. Replay supports accountability, institutional learning, model evaluation, and identification of avoidable versus irreducible uncertainty.

## 19  Trust and Provenance Layer
Trust in The Eye is not a confidence badge. It is the inspectable chain connecting source, transformation, inference, approval, decision, action, and outcome.
## Trust envelope
Every intelligence object shall carry a trust envelope appropriate to its type. Trust policy shall combine source authority, corroboration, recency, method fitness, lineage integrity, conflict, evaluation, and human review rather than assigning permanent universal reputation.
## Audit and non-repudiation
Material reads, writes, transformations, exports, policy decisions, agent tool calls, model invocations, approvals, overrides, releases, and administrative actions shall be attributable and tamper-evident. Audit records must be searchable by object, identity, decision, tenant, policy, and time, with retention and disclosure controlled separately from operational data.

## 21  Security, Privacy, Sovereignty, and Resilience
=== PARA 301 ===
The Eye will hold the evidence, models, assumptions, and decisions an institution considers most sensitive. Its security architecture must therefore protect not only data, but strategic intent and decision integrity.
## Zero-trust operating model
Every human, service, device, workload, agent, model, connector, source, and deployment component shall have verifiable identity and scoped authority. Access decisions shall combine role, attributes, purpose, tenant, classification, data policy, device and workload posture, network context, and risk. ## Data and model protection
Protection shall include encryption in transit and at rest, customer-managed keys where required, secrets isolation, field- and row-level controls, confidential-computing options where appropriate, secure deletion, backup protection, exfiltration controls, watermarking or export controls for sensitive products, and isolation of training and evaluation data. The platform shall be designed for fault containment, redundancy, backup, restore, disaster recovery, cyber recovery, dependency failure, model outage, connector degradation, and disconnected operation. ## 23  Continuous Learning and Evaluation
The Eye learns continuously through governed evidence and outcomes. It does not learn by silently converting every interaction into truth or every model suggestion into production behavior.
## Learning loop
Capture the original observation, prediction, scenario, simulation, recommendation, human decision, action, and measurable outcome.
Evaluate performance against explicit baselines and metrics, segmented by context, horizon, domain, confidence, and operating regime.
Diagnose error sources across data, ontology, extraction, reasoning, model, twin, assumption, workflow, policy, and human use.
Propose changes as versioned artifacts with expected benefit, risk, evidence, test plan, compatibility impact, and rollback path.
Evaluate offline and in controlled environments, including regression, adversarial, security, privacy, bias, calibration, and cost tests.
Approve, release progressively, monitor, compare, and roll back when performance or policy deviates from the accepted envelope.
## Feedback is evidence, not truth

Feedback shall retain source, role, context, and outcome and shall be evaluated before changing production behavior. A popular answer is not necessarily a correct or responsible answer.

Evaluation shall cover collection fidelity, extraction, entity resolution, relationship accuracy, retrieval, summarization, graph quality, forecast calibration, scenario coverage, simulation validity, recommendation quality, explanation usefulness, agent task success, tool safety, policy compliance, robustness, bias, latency, cost, and operator workload. Evaluation datasets and graders shall be versioned and protected from contamination.
## Tenant isolation
Customer data and feedback shall not be used to train shared models or improve other tenants without explicit authority, legal basis, contractual permission, and technical separation. Federated or privacy-preserving learning may be supported, but participation and contribution boundaries must be inspectable.

## Universal source obligations
Authorization, rights, licensing, purpose, classification, and data-residency policy are known before use.
Original evidence and source metadata are preserved according to policy.
Event, publication, collection, effective, and system-record time are captured where relevant.
Connector, parser, schema, transformation, and content versions are traceable.
Freshness, completeness, quality, health, failure, and coverage gaps are measurable.
Untrusted content is isolated from agent tools, policy, credentials, and cross-tenant context.
Downstream objects can be identified when a source is corrected, withdrawn, retracted, or prohibited.
