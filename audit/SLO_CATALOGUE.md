# SLO catalogue — floors and declared variance across the deployment profiles (v1)

Decision: register §8 R-4 (3), owner 2026-09-10 — "SLO baselines: safety/durability/provenance semantics and floors preserved without weakening, with expressly declared and justified operational variance where the controlling clauses permit it, mapped for the ENTIRE catalogue — P7-D". Source catalogue: Volume 4 Appendix F "Quality and SLO Catalog" (p. 183; `audit/extraction/text/v04.txt` L9631–L9719), 22 objectives SLO-001..022, register rows `V4:SLO-001..022` (`audit/requirements/v04.csv` lines 412–433), acceptance units AU-INF-0448..0469 (evidence class `operating-measurement`, package P7-D, profiles `saas|private|onprem`).

## 1. The controlling clauses

- **Meaning is identical on every profile.** V8 PAR-14 (p. 193): "Reliability state — Identical SLO meaning, product state, degradation, recovery, and evidence"; V0 C-042: "same core semantics … with documented capability differences only where unavoidable"; V6 IA-03-003 (p. 14): "provider, topology, operating model, and locality may vary; authority, object, workflow, failure, audit, replay, and human-decision semantics may not"; PR-65-003 (p. 147): "deployment choice changes operational responsibility and topology, not human authority, object meaning, correction, explanation, export, replay, or core product behavior".
- **What may vary, and only when declared.** V4 Appendix F preamble (p. 183): "Component owners refine populations and targets by consequence and deployment profile without weakening constitutional safety or product transparency"; V8 PAR-14 permitted variance: "Topology and service objective values"; PR-CC-004 (every chapter): "operational variance SHALL be declared"; PR-65-002 (p. 147): the product persists and reconciles "service level, limitation, variance … parity status"; V6 IA-03-002 (p. 14): every instance declares "permitted physical variance".
- **What may never weaken.** V4 Appendix F SLO RULE (p. 183): "An error budget controls change velocity; it does not authorize loss of human decision sovereignty, provenance, tenant isolation, policy enforcement, audit, temporal truth, or deployment semantic parity." V0 C-043: "fail safely, degrade intelligibly, recover predictably, preserve evidence and audit continuity". PR-65-005 (p. 148): a profile that cannot pass is marked "unavailable or explicitly non-parity".
- **When a measurement counts.** V6 IA-59-003 (p. 133): "an SLO is valid only when the measurement covers the user-visible product and explicit partial, stale, denied, and degraded states." V3 V03-T-437 (p. 91): "Platform SLOs govern technical services. Product SLOs govern the quality and timeliness of evidence, graph, twin, forecast, warning, decision, and briefing products".

## 2. Classes

- **F — floor.** The baseline objective value, window and breach behaviour hold on every leg (SaaS, Private Cloud, On-Premise; disconnected and air-gapped where the unit carries those legs). No declared variance can lower the value or change the breach behaviour; a profile that cannot meet it is non-parity for the capability (PR-65-005), never "accepted with variance".
- **V — value variance permitted.** The indicator, measured condition and breach behaviour are floors; the numeric objective (percentile, time bound, availability figure) may be declared per profile under PAR-14 "service objective values" and the Appendix F preamble, with the record of §3. Weakening the breach behaviour (e.g. serving stale reads as current) is never variance — it is the semantic PR-CC-004 keeps identical.
- **P — population-defined target.** The catalogue itself sets the target per population ("Per source", "Per twin class", "Per horizon", "Per profile", "Per severity"). The declared population target is the objective on each leg; a profile declares its population targets in the same §3 record; meaning and breach behaviour are floors.

## 3. The declared-variance record (one per leg, per SLO; required before a V or P leg is accepted)

`slo_id`, `leg`, `baseline` (Appendix F value), `declared` (the leg's value), `justification` (topology / provider / locality / sync window — never "customer preference"), `owner`, `effective_from`, `review_by`, `breach_behaviour` (must equal the catalogue's), `measurement` (IA-59-003: user-visible product, partial/stale/denied/degraded states counted), `parity_status` (PR-65-002), `evidence` (the signed measurement for the exact release, deployment and period — ADR-023). Recorded in the PR-65-002 object when it exists; until then in `docs/acceptance/<leg>/slo/<slo_id>.md` and pointed to by the unit's `legs_verified` entry.

## 5. How a leg of an SLO unit is accepted (B4)

For AU-INF-0448..0469, `legs_verified` names, per leg, the signed measurement for the exact release, deployment and period (ADR-023; ES-14-005 "signed result for the exact provider, consumer, schema, and environment"): an F leg is accepted when the measured value meets the baseline and the breach behaviour was exercised; a V or P leg when the measured value meets the value in the leg's §3 record and the breach behaviour was exercised; a leg with no §3 record for a V/P objective is not accepted. `verified:all` follows the B4 rule (every leg). The unit's `condition` is set to `class F|V|P per audit/SLO_CATALOGUE.md §4` (a mechanical edit of the 22 units; counts unchanged).
