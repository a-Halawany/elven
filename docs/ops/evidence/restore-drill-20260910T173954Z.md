## 8. Limitations of this drill

1. The live containers were deliberately left on the previous official images;
   the drill proves that a bundle taken from them restores and serves on the
   published digests with the protections. The governed recreation of
   `eye-postgres` / `eye-redis` onto the pins is a separate recorded operation;
   until it happens, every backup will record `pin_digest_matches: false` and
   `running_protections_match: false`, and every restore will print the REPORT
   line of §3.
2. The postgres image's local `RepoDigests` carry a second digest
   (`sha256:d3dd485b…`, the arm64 platform manifest of the same index, from an
   earlier pull on this host). The check requires the pin's digest to be
   carried, which it is; it does not require it to be the only one.
3. `pg_restore` of `eye_demo` took 3 s (1 s in the second drill) and the
   vault tar is 64.6 MB (43.9 MB): the demonstration grew by an order of
   magnitude in one day. The RTO figures of the runbook's §9 are updated.
4. Everything the runbook lists in §10 still holds: the harness database's
   absent blobs, the frozen fixture partitions, the three capture moments, the
   expected globals error, the empty journal, the restored API's own writes,
   the unencrypted bundle.
5. The runs of the day before the drill of record (bundle `20260910T173834Z`,
   root `20260910T173907Z`) are left in place under `$HOME`; they are not the
   drill of record.
